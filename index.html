协议体检：原版 vs 仿盘

看一个协议的账，先看它的控制权结构。原版的优点和它自己的红线放在同一张表里；仿盘的问题全部来自链上实读。

原版 · TapeOut / BEM（BSC）— 优势

  • 挖矿合约已于 2026-09-14 封印；排放固定 7,200 BEM/天，减半路径已公布至 2030-08-19。
  • 真实、可测的经济体：21,081 台机器、总权重 1,650,392 H，主池流动性约 $151K（头部池合计约 $282K，2026-11-16）。
  • 铸造元件的协议费 50% 销毁，规则写在合约里。
  • 审计沿革存在：Salus 审计过 v2.0.0（报告 2026-10-28）。
  • 官方矿池一期已上线出产（约 173.6 BEM/天）；二期募集披露与链上实读一致（477 枚 NAND 对 477）。
  • 官方界面与合约中未发现推荐 / 多级返利机制。

原版 · BEM — 它自己的红线（不藏）

  • 工厂合约仍可升级、由团队 3/5 多签持有——与白皮书「所有权完全放弃」的说法不符。
  • 现行 v2.3.0 未经外部复审（Salus 只覆盖 v2.0.0，8 项发现为自称已修复、仍待确认）。
  • 排放是通胀的：累计销毁 2,499.84 BEM，不足一周排放量的 5%，没有通缩对冲。
  • 官方矿池注册矿工数仍为 0；币价波动极大（上次检查 30 天 −73.4%）。
  • 团队匿名。

仿盘 · FabX / FAB（X Layer）— 结构病

  • 约 87.3% 总供应在创世时预铸给部署者的个人地址（EOA），无锁仓。
  • 挖矿合约可升级、未封印，owner 是个人私钥——规则可被单方随时更改。
  • 无审计、无公布的减半表、团队匿名。
  • 2026-10-13 才上线；7,006 台矿机 / 732 个持有人，厚度比原版低一个数量级，池流动性约 $131K。

三条误传更正

  • 误传：「仿盘是官方 X Layer 版」——不是。社区仿盘，无官方背书。
  • 误传：「原版合约全都改不了」——挖矿合约已封印，但工厂合约仍可升级（团队 3/5 多签）。
  • 误传：「仿盘筹码分散、去中心化」——87.3% 的供应在一个部署者地址手里。

Original · TapeOut / BEM (BSC) — strengths

  • Mining contract sealed on 2026-09-14; emissions fixed at 7,200 BEM/day on a published halving path to 2030-08-19.
  • A real, measurable economy: 21,081 circuits, 1,650,392 H total weight, main pool liquidity ≈ $151K (≈ $282K across the top pools, 2026-11-16).
  • 50% of the component-minting protocol fee is burned, enforced in the contract.
  • Audit lineage exists: Salus audited v2.0.0 (report dated 2026-10-28).
  • Official pool phase 1 is live and producing (≈ 173.6 BEM/day); phase-2 raise disclosures match on-chain reads exactly (477 NAND read vs 477 disclosed).
  • No referral or multi-level rebate mechanism found anywhere in the official surfaces or contracts.

Original · BEM — its own red lines (stated, not hidden)

  • The factory contract is still upgradeable, held by a team 3/5 multisig — contradicting the whitepaper's "ownership fully renounced" framing.
  • The live v2.3.0 code has not been externally re-audited (Salus covered v2.0.0 only; the 8 findings are self-declared fixed, still pending).
  • Emissions are inflationary: cumulative burns (2,499.84 BEM) are under 5% of a single week's emissions — no deflationary offset.
  • The official pool still shows 0 registered miners; the token price is extremely volatile (−73.4% in 30 days at last check).
  • The team is anonymous.

Clone · FabX / FAB (X Layer) — structural flaws

  • ≈ 87.3% of total supply pre-minted at genesis to the deployer's personal EOA, with no lock-up.
  • Mining contract upgradeable and unsealed, owned by a personal private key — the rules can change unilaterally at any time.
  • No audit, no published halving schedule, anonymous team.
  • Launched 2026-10-13; 7,006 miners / 732 holders — an order of magnitude thinner than the original; pool liquidity ≈ $131K.

Three myth corrections

  • Myth: "The clone is the official X Layer version." — It is not. A community clone with no official endorsement.
  • Myth: "The original's contracts can't be changed at all." — The mining contract is sealed, but the factory contract remains upgradeable (team 3/5 multisig).
  • Myth: "The clone's supply is distributed and decentralized." — 87.3% of supply sits in a single deployer address.
